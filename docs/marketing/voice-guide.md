# roomsie voice guide

**Status:** starter draft. Devashish completes it in MK-03, and Yash approves it.

This guide is for posts, ads, captions, messages and articles. It is not STE.

## How roomsie sounds

- Like a friend from Mumbai who already went through the flat hunt. Not a portal, not a broker.
- Hinglish in posts and ads. Plain English in articles.
- Short lines. One idea in each post.
- Specific: real areas, real rupee amounts, real situations.

## Sample lines (replace with your own)

- "Flat mil gaya, flatmate nahi? Bas 5 minute baat karo, roomsie dikhayega kaun fit hai."
- "Broker ko ek mahina ka rent? Pehle yeh padh lo."
- "Sleep schedule ulta, kitchen alag, guests ka rule? Yeh sab pehle hi pooch lete hain."

## Words to use

flatmate, flat, Mumbai, area names, rent, deposit, "baat karo", "fit"

## Words to avoid

seamless, revolutionary, delve, navigate, leverage, robust, "in today's fast-paced world"

## Claims we never make at launch

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Verified profiles" | Verification is v1 (PD8) |
| "Women-only" or "safe for women only" | roomsie is open to all genders (PD1) |
| "Thousands of users", "Mumbai's #1" | Not true before launch; also a misleading-claim risk |
| "Refer and earn" | Referrals are v1 |
| Prices or plans | v0 is free; never say "free forever" |

## Before you publish, check

1. Is each claim true today?
2. Does it name a real place or number?
3. Would a 24-year-old in Andheri say it like this?
