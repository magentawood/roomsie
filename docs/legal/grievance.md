# Grievance Officer

**Effective from:** 8 November 2026

If you have a complaint about roomsie, about content on roomsie, or about your data, write to our Grievance Officer.

**Grievance Officer:** Niruv Bhardwaj
**Email:** roomsie.in@gmail.com

## How to complain

Send an email with:

- Your name, and the email address of your roomsie account if you have one.
- What the complaint is about, with a link to the card, the listing or the conversation if there is one.
- What you want us to do.

To use a right of the [privacy policy](privacy.md), for example to get a copy of your data, write from the email of your roomsie account. Thus we know that it is you.

## How long it takes

- We tell you that we got your complaint within **24 hours**.
- We resolve it within **7 days**.
- If you ask us to remove content that breaks the law or our [terms](terms.md), we act within **36 hours**.
- If the content shows a person in an intimate way, or pretends to be a person, we remove it within **2 hours**.

These are the times of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, as amended in February 2026.

## If you are not happy with the answer

- About content or an account: you can appeal to the Grievance Appellate Committee of the Government of India within 30 days of our answer.
- About your personal data: you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## You can also report on roomsie

When you are signed in, "Report" on the page of a person or a listing, or in a conversation, sends your report to our moderators. The [terms](terms.md) tell how a report works.
