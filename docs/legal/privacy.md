# Privacy policy

**Effective from:** 8 November 2026

This policy tells you what roomsie collects, why, how long we keep it, who else gets it, and how you can see, change or delete it. roomsie is a service to find a flat, a room or a flatmate in Mumbai. In this policy, "we" and "roomsie" mean the team that runs roomsie.in.

roomsie is only for people of 18 or more.

## What we collect, and why

**When you chat without an account**

- **Your chat with the roomsie assistant:** what you type, what you tap, and the replies. We use it to understand what you look for and to show results.
- **A visitor key:** a random key that your browser keeps, so that your chat continues when you come back. We keep only a hash of it.
- **Hashes of your network:** a hash of your IP address, of its network range, and of your IP address together with the name and version of your browser. We never keep the address itself. We use them to stop abuse, for example a script that opens many chats.

**When you sign in**

- **Your sign-in:** you sign in with Google. We keep your Google email address and a user id from our sign-in service. We use the email to send you alerts, and to know who you are when you ask us something.
- **Your profile:** your name, age, gender, job (the role only, never the company), commute address, the photos that you add (at most four), what you tell others about yourself, and what you look for: the area, the budget, the move date, your lifestyle and your must-haves. Other people use your card to decide if they want to live with you.
- **Two optional fields:** your religion and your community. They are never on your card. We use them only to match you with people who ask for it, as the next section says.
- **Your listings:** the place, its rent and deposit, its address if you choose to share it, its photos, and the flatmates that you add. You must have the consent of each flatmate before you add their name, age, photo or other details.
- **Your chats with the assistant:** as for a visitor. The assistant also keeps short notes of what you told it about yourself, for example that you prefer a quiet flat, so that it does not ask again. The chat shows each note, and you can delete it there.
- **Your messages:** the requests and the messages that you send to other people on roomsie.
- **Your ID check, if you do it:** a photo of a government ID (never Aadhaar) and a selfie. A machine compares the two faces, and a person of our team checks the result. We delete the two photos right after the review, and at most 7 days after the upload. We keep only the type of the ID, the face match score, the result, and the time of the review.
- **Reports and blocks:** the reports that you send or that others send about you, and the people that you block.
- **Feedback:** the face and the text that you send us.
- **Alerts:** a token of your browser if you turn on browser alerts.
- **The waitlist:** your email and the place, if you ask us to tell you when roomsie opens in a place outside Mumbai.

**About how roomsie is used**

- **Events:** for example "a chat started" or "a request was accepted", with a random id that is not your user id. Events hold no text that you wrote. We use them to count how roomsie is used.
- **Error reports:** when something breaks, the error and your user id, with no message text.
- **Records of the assistant:** for each answer of the assistant, the text that went to the model and its raw answer. We keep them to find out why an answer was wrong. Before we save them, we replace each word of religion and each community name that is not a language with "[hidden]". They carry a session code, never your name or email. Each time a moderator opens one, we log who opened it and when.

## The religion and community filter

You can tell the assistant that you want flatmates of a religion or a community, for example "only Jain" or "preferably Marathi". If you do, roomsie filters on what other people stated about themselves. We never guess a religion or a community from a name, a diet, an area or a festival. We never suggest such a filter. The people that a filter leaves out are never told, and nobody sees the filter. A person who stated nothing stays in the results. We have no caste filter.

## Who can see what

- **Anyone, also with no account:** the results cards. A person card shows the first photo, the name, the age, the job, what the person looks for, the budget, the move date, a lifestyle line and the bio. A place card shows the first photo, the title, the area and the rent.
- **Only people who signed in:** the full card or listing, with the commute address, all the photos, the description and the flatmates.
- **The person you write to:** your messages. Before an accept, only your first note.
- You can hide your card in Settings. A card that a parent runs for an adult son or daughter always says "Managed by a parent".
- Phone numbers: we mask each phone number in names, bios, listings and request notes before we save them. After an accept, two people can share numbers in their chat.
- **Our team:** the moderators see what they need to review a report, and the email of a person. The ID reviewers see the photos of an ID check until we delete them.

## Who else gets your data

We use these services to run roomsie. Each one gets only what it needs.

| Service | What it gets | Where |
|---|---|---|
| DeepSeek | The chat text and what the assistant knows of your search, to write the replies | China |
| Google Gemini | The same, when DeepSeek fails or is slow | Where Google has servers |
| Google Firebase | Your sign-in, and the alerts to your browser | Where Google has servers |
| Supabase | Our databases, and the live updates of your messages | Mumbai |
| Amazon Web Services | Our server, the email alerts, and the files of an ID check and their face check | Mumbai |
| Cloudflare | The photos, and our encrypted backups | Cloudflare's network |
| Vercel | The web pages | Mumbai |
| Sentry | The error reports | Sentry's servers |
| GitHub | Our nightly backup job, which encrypts the copy of our databases before it stores it | GitHub's servers |

- **DeepSeek** keeps the text in China, and its terms give no end date. Its terms let it use the text, in a de-identified form, to improve its models. We asked DeepSeek to opt us out of that.
- **Google Gemini** runs on our paid account. Google does not use the text to train its models. It keeps the text for 55 days to find abuse.
- We do not sell your data, and we show no ads.

## How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| The chat of a visitor | 30 days after the last visit. A nightly job deletes it, so at most 31 days. If you sign in, the chat moves to your account. |
| Your account: profile, photos, listings, chats with the assistant, notes, saves, feedback and alerts | Until you delete your account |
| Your messages and request notes to other people | They stay with the other person after you delete your account. They show "Deleted user" as the sender. |
| Your sign-in email, the date you joined, and the date you left | 180 days after you delete your account, in a locked record, as the Indian IT Rules ask. Then we delete it. |
| The photos of an ID check | Deleted right after the review, at most 7 days |
| The result of an ID check, and the log of who saw it | Until you delete your account |
| Reports, blocks and the moderation log | They stay after you delete your account, so that the moderators can act on abuse |
| The records of the assistant | 30 days, and at once when you delete your account |
| Events | 12 months, deleted by whole month, so at most 13 months. When you delete your account, they lose the link to you. |
| Daily counts of the events | No end. They hold counts and no person. |
| Error reports | 30 days |
| The waitlist | For an account, until you delete your account. For a visitor, until the chat goes and we tell you that roomsie opened in your place. |
| Our backups | 14 days |
| A deleted photo | A copy can stay in the Cloudflare network for 10 more minutes. |
| The text at DeepSeek and Google | Under their own terms, as the table above says. Each week, we ask both to delete the text of the accounts that were deleted that week. They have no tool to find the text of one person, so they may not be able to do it. |

## Your browser

roomsie sets no cookies. Your browser keeps:

- your visitor key, your last chat and the waitlist places that you joined, until you sign out or clear your browser;
- your sign-in and your browser alerts, kept by Google's sign-in and alert services;
- a few settings of the page, until you close the tab.

## Your rights

- **See your data:** most of it is in your profile, your listings and your chats. For a copy of the rest, email us.
- **Change it:** edit your profile and your listings at any time.
- **Delete it:** Settings, then Delete account. It deletes what the table above says, at once.
- **Withdraw consent:** delete an optional field, a note, a photo or your account. Turn off email or browser alerts in Settings.
- **Name a nominee:** a person who can use these rights for you if you die or cannot act. Email us.
- **Complain:** email our Grievance Officer (below). If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## If something goes wrong

If your data leaks, we tell you and the Data Protection Board as soon as we can.

## Children

roomsie is only for people of 18 or more. A parent can search for a child under 18, but the child gets no card. If you think that a person under 18 has a card, report it: we hide the card at once and review it.

## Changes

When we change this policy, we change the date at the top and tell you on roomsie or by email. We remind each user of this policy once a year.
