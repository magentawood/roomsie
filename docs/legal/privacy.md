# Privacy policy

**Effective from:** 8 November 2026

roomsie is a service to find a flat, a room or a flatmate in Mumbai. In this policy, "we" and "roomsie" mean the team that runs roomsie.in.

## What we collect, and why

**When you chat without an account**

- **Your chat with the roomsie assistant:** what you type, what you tap, and the replies. We use it to understand what you look for and to show results.
- **A visitor key:** a random key that your browser keeps, so that your chat continues when you come back. We keep only a hash of it.
- **Hashes of your network:** a hash of your IP address and a hash of its network range. Also a hash of your IP address together with the user agent of your browser: its name, its version and your system. We never keep the address itself. We use them to stop abuse, for example a script that opens many chats.
- **What you give us in the chat:** the notes of the assistant, your feedback, an email for the waitlist, and your religion or community if you state them. If you ask for flatmates of a religion or a community, each message that asks for it keeps that request. The next parts tell what each one is for.

**When you sign in**

- **Your sign-in:** you sign in with Google. We keep your Google email address and a user id from our sign-in service. We use the email to send you alerts, and to know who you are when you ask us something.
- **Your profile:** your name, age, gender and job (the role only, never the company). Also your commute address, the photos that you add (at most four), what you tell others about yourself, and what you look for. What you look for is the area, the budget, the move date, your lifestyle and your must-haves. Other people use your card to decide if they want to live with you. People who signed in see your commute address, to judge the travel.
- **Your religion and your community, if you give them:** in two optional fields of your profile, or when you state them in the chat. The newer statement replaces the older one. Only you see them, and they are never on your card. We use them only to match you with people who ask for it, as the next section says.
- **Your listings:** the place, its rent and deposit, its address, its photos, and the flatmates that you add. Other people always see the area, the building and the landmark. They see the street address and the pincode only if you choose to share them. We use your listings to show your place to the people who search. You must have the consent of each flatmate before you add their name, age, photo or other details.
- **Your chats with the assistant:** as for a visitor. The assistant also keeps short notes of what you told it about yourself, for example that you prefer a quiet flat. It uses them so that it does not ask again, and to order your results. Ask "What do you know about me?" in the chat to see your notes, and delete any of them there.
- **Your messages:** the requests and the messages that you send to other people on roomsie. We use them to deliver your words to the other person.
- **Your ID check, if you do it:** a photo of a government ID (never Aadhaar) and a selfie. We use them to show the "Verified" mark on your card. A machine compares the two faces, and a person of our team checks the result. We keep the type of the ID, the result, the reason of a rejection, the note of the reviewer, who reviewed it and when. We also keep a log of each view of the photos and each decision.
- **Reports and blocks:** the reports that you send or that others send about you, and the people that you block. We use them to keep people safe. A block stops a person from reaching you.
- **Feedback:** the face and the text that you send us. We use it to make roomsie better.
- **Alerts:** a token of your browser if you turn on browser alerts. We use it to send you the alerts.
- **The waitlist:** your email and the place, if you ask us to tell you when roomsie opens in a place outside Mumbai. We use it only for that.

**About how roomsie is used**

- **Events:** for example "a chat started" or "a request was accepted", with a random id that is not your user id. Events hold no text that you wrote. We use them to count how roomsie is used.
- **Error reports:** when something breaks, the error, your user id and the user agent of your browser, with no message text. A report of a blocked page part also has the address of the page. We use them to fix roomsie.
- **Records of the assistant:** for each answer of the assistant, the text that went to the model and its raw answer. We keep them to find out why an answer was wrong. Before we save them, we replace each word of religion and each community name that is not a language with "[hidden]". They carry a session code, never your name or email. Each time a moderator opens one, we log who opened it and when.

## The religion and community filter

You can tell the assistant that you want flatmates of a religion or a community, for example "only Jain" or "preferably Marathi". If you do, roomsie filters on what other people stated about themselves. We never guess a religion or a community from a name, a diet, an area or a festival. We never suggest such a filter. The people that a filter leaves out are never told, and no other user sees the filter. A person who stated nothing stays in the results. We have no caste filter.

## Who can see what

- **Anyone, also with no account:** the results cards. A person card shows the first photo, the name, the age, the job, what the person looks for, the budget, the move date, a lifestyle line and the bio. A place card shows the first photo, the title, the area and the rent.
- **Only people who signed in:** the full card or listing, with the commute address, all the photos, the description and the flatmates.
- **The person you write to:** your messages. Before an accept, only your first note.
- You can hide your card in Settings. A card that a parent runs for an adult son or daughter always says "Managed by a parent".
- Phone numbers: we mask each phone number in names, bios, listings and request notes before we save them. After an accept, two people can share numbers in their chat.
- **Our moderators:** they can find any account by its name or email, and they see what they need to review a report. They can open the records of the assistant. The ID reviewers see the photos of an ID check until we delete them.

## Who else gets your data

We use these services to run roomsie. Each one gets only what it needs.

| Service | What it gets | Where |
|---|---|---|
| DeepSeek | The chat text and what the assistant knows of your search, to write the replies | China |
| Google Gemini | The same, when DeepSeek fails or is slow | Many countries, also outside India |
| Google Firebase | Your sign-in, and the alerts to your browser | Many countries, also outside India |
| Supabase | Our databases, and the live updates of your messages | Mumbai |
| Amazon Web Services | Our server, the email alerts, and the files of an ID check and their face check | Mumbai |
| Cloudflare | The photos, and our encrypted backups | Many countries |
| Vercel | The web pages. Its functions run in Mumbai. | Mumbai, and a network in many countries |
| Sentry | The error reports | Can be outside India |
| GitHub | Our nightly backup job. The job copies our databases, encrypts the copy and stores it in Cloudflare. The plain copy exists only while the job runs. | Can be outside India |

- **DeepSeek** keeps the text in China, and its terms give no end date. Its terms let it use the text, in a de-identified form, to improve its models. We ask DeepSeek to opt us out of that.
- **Google Gemini** runs on our paid account. Google does not use the text to train its models. It keeps the text for 55 days to find abuse.
- We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

| Data | How long |
|---|---|
| The chat of a visitor, with its visitor key and the hashes of its network | At most 31 days after the last visit. If you sign in, the chat moves to your account. |
| The hashes of your network and your visitor key, after you sign in | Until you delete your account |
| Your account: profile, photos, listings, chats with the assistant, notes, saves, feedback and alerts | Until you delete your account |
| Your messages and request notes to other people | They stay with the other person after you delete your account. They show "Deleted user" as the sender. |
| Your sign-in email | 180 days after you delete your account, in a locked record with the date you joined and the date you left, as the Indian IT Rules ask |
| The record of a deleted account: its dates, if it was suspended, and a code made from your Google account id | The dates stay with no end. The code is a hash, not the id. It stops a suspended or reported person from making a new account. It goes when you join again with the same Google account. |
| The photos of an ID check | Deleted right after the review, at most 7 days |
| The result of an ID check, and the log of who saw it | Until you delete your account |
| Reports, blocks and the moderation log | No end. They stay after you delete your account, so that the moderators can act on abuse. |
| The records of the assistant | 30 days, and at once when you delete your account |
| The log of who opened the records of the assistant | No end. It holds a session code, never your words. |
| Events | 12 months, deleted by whole month, so at most 13 months and one day. When you delete your account, they lose the link to you. |
| Daily counts of the events | No end. They hold counts and no person. |
| A quote of yours on our home page, if you agreed to it | Until a moderator removes it, or you ask us to remove it |
| An invite to roomsie before the launch: the email | Until a moderator removes it |
| Error reports | 30 days |
| The waitlist | For an account, until you delete your account. For a visitor, until the chat goes and we tell you that roomsie opened in your place. |
| Our backups | 14 days |
| A deleted photo | A copy can stay in the Cloudflare network for 10 more minutes. |
| The text at DeepSeek and Google | Under their own terms, as the list above says. Each week, we ask both to delete the text of the accounts that were deleted that week. They have no tool to find the text of one person, so possibly they cannot do it. |

## Your browser

roomsie sets no cookies. Your browser keeps these:

- Your visitor key, until you sign in.
- The id of your last chat and the waitlist places that you joined, until you sign out or clear your browser.
- Your sign-in and your browser alerts, kept by Google's sign-in and alert services.
- A few settings of the page, until you close the tab.

## Your rights

- **See your data:** most of it is in your profile, your listings and your chats. For a copy of the rest, email us.
- **Change it:** edit your profile and your listings at any time.
- **Delete it:** Profile and Settings, then Delete my account. It deletes what the table above says, at once.
- **Withdraw consent:** delete an optional field, a note, a photo or your account. A religion or community that you stated in a chat stays with that chat until you delete your account. Turn off email or browser alerts in Settings.
- **Name a nominee:** a person who can use these rights for you if you die or cannot act. Email us.
- **Complain:** email our Grievance Officer (below). If you are not happy with the answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## If something goes wrong

If your data leaks, we tell you and the Data Protection Board as soon as we can.

## Children

roomsie is only for people of 18 or more. A parent can search for a child under 18, but the child gets no card. If you think that a person under 18 has a card, report it. We hide the card at once and review it.

## Changes

When we change this policy, we change the date at the top and tell you on roomsie or by email. We remind each user of this policy once a year.
