# Terms of use

**Effective from:** 8 November 2026

These terms are the agreement between you and roomsie when you use roomsie.in. In these terms, "we" and "roomsie" mean the team that runs roomsie.in. If you do not agree to them, do not use roomsie.

## What roomsie is

- roomsie helps you find a flat, a room or a flatmate in Mumbai. An assistant asks what you look for, then shows places and people. You can message the people and the listers on roomsie.
- **roomsie is not a broker or an agent.** We do not own, rent or check the places. We do not take part in a rent, a deposit or a rental agreement. The agreement is between you and the other person.
- **The "Verified" mark** means that a person of our team compared the selfie of the person with the photo of a government ID. It is not a check of their past, of their job, or of the place.
- **The assistant can be wrong.** Check each fact yourself before you decide. On law, tax or the safety of an area, it gives general information, not advice.

## Who can use roomsie

- You must be 18 or more.
- A parent can make a card for a son or daughter of 18 or more. The card says "Managed by a parent", and the parent sends and gets the messages.
- You sign in with your own Google account. Keep it safe. You are responsible for what you and others do with your account.
- If we suspended your account, do not make a new one.

## What you post

You are responsible for your profile, your listings, your photos and your messages. When you post:

- Tell the truth. Post only a place that you can rent out or share, and only photos of it.
- Post photos only of yourself, or of people who agreed. Before you add a flatmate to a listing, get their consent.
- Do not put a phone number or a WhatsApp link in your name, bio, listing or request note. We mask them there. After the other person accepts your request, you can share a number in your chat.
- Do not write text that keeps out people by community, religion or caste. A listing can say who is welcome (for example students or families) and who can move in (men, women or any), with the choices that roomsie gives.
- Do not harass, threaten or insult a person.
- Do not send spam.
- Do not post anything that Indian law forbids. For example, do not post content that:
  - is obscene or harms a child.
  - pretends to be another person.
  - breaks a copyright, or misleads on purpose.
  - threatens the unity, security or public order of India.

You keep your rights in what you post. You let roomsie show it, store it and copy it as roomsie needs to run the service. This ends when you delete it, except for the cases in the [privacy policy](privacy.md), for example your messages to other people.

## Messages

- Your first message to a person is a request, with a note of up to 300 characters. You can write more after they accept.
- You can send 10 new requests a day. A request ends after 14 days.

## Reports and what we can do

- A signed-in person can report a person or a listing from its page or from a conversation. The reasons: a fake profile or listing, a broker or spam, harassment, discriminatory text, a person under 18, shared contact details, or other.
- Some reports hide the card or the listing at once, until a moderator reviews it. These are a report of harassment or of a person under 18, and reports from two different people.
- A moderator reviews a report of harassment or of a person under 18 within 4 hours, and other reports within 24 hours.
- If you break these terms, we can warn you, hide or remove what you posted, or suspend your account. We can do this without notice.
- If you break the law, you can face penalties under that law. We must report some offences to the authorities, and we give information to the police or a court when the law asks us.

## Limits

To stop abuse, roomsie limits how much a person without an account can chat, how many chats a network can start, and how fast a person can send. When you meet a limit, roomsie tells you what to do.

## Price

roomsie is free at this time. Before we ask for money for anything, we tell you, and you choose.

## Our responsibility

- We give roomsie as it is. We do our best to keep it safe and in service. We do not promise that it always works, or that a place or a person is what they say.
- Meet people in a safe place.
- Do not pay a deposit before you see the place and the papers.
- As far as the law allows, roomsie is not responsible for a loss from an agreement between users. The same is true for a loss from what a user posted, or from a fact that the assistant gave.

## Ending

- You can delete your account at any time: Profile and Settings, then Delete my account. The [privacy policy](privacy.md) tells what goes and what stays.
- We can end roomsie or a part of it. If we do, we tell you first when we can.

## Changes

When we change these terms, we change the date at the top and tell you on roomsie or by email. If you use roomsie after the change, you agree to the new terms. We remind each user of these terms once a year.

## Law and disputes

Indian law applies to these terms. The courts of Mumbai decide a dispute.
